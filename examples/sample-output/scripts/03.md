# Script 03 — Splitting rent, groceries and bills with roommates without conflict

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 short
**Target runtime:** 50 seconds

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:02)

**Voiceover:** "Splitting bills with roommates goes wrong for one reason."

**On-screen text:** SPLITTING BILLS WITH ROOMMATES

**Visual:** Two people at a kitchen table, one holding a phone showing a payment app, both looking at the screen.

---

## BEAT 1 (0:02–0:10)

**Voiceover:** "You split the total. But you don't split the use. Someone's home more. Someone runs the heat. Someone eats the snacks."

**On-screen text:** SPLIT THE TOTAL ≠ SPLIT THE USE

**Visual:** Split screen — one roommate at a desk all day, another walking out the door; a hand reaching into a shared snack shelf.

---

## BEAT 2 (0:10–0:19)

**Voiceover:** "So don't split everything equally. Split rent by room size. Split utilities by headcount. Split groceries by what each person actually buys."

**On-screen text:** RENT BY ROOM · UTILITIES BY PERSON · GROCERIES BY RECEIPT

**Visual:** Top-down shot of a floor plan with three rooms labeled by size; three names written next to a utility bill.

---

## BEAT 3 (0:19–0:28)

**Voiceover:** "Pick one app for the shared stuff. One place for rent, one place for utilities, one place for the grocery run. Not four group chats and a whiteboard."

**On-screen text:** ONE APP. ONE PLACE.

**Visual:** Phone screen showing a single shared expenses app; a group chat with unread messages fades out of frame.

---

## BEAT 4 (0:28–0:37)

**Voiceover:** "Write it down before you sign the lease. Who pays what, on what day, and what happens when someone's late. Two minutes now saves a semester of arguing."

**On-screen text:** AGREE BEFORE YOU SIGN

**Visual:** A simple one-page written agreement on the table next to a lease document; a pen resting on top.

---

## BEAT 5 (0:37–0:45)

**Voiceover:** "And say the awkward thing early. 'Hey, I'm short this month.' That's a lot easier than silence for three weeks."

**On-screen text:** SAY IT EARLY

**Visual:** Two roommates on a couch, one talking, the other nodding; no receipts or phones in frame.

---

## CTA (0:45–0:50)

**Voiceover:** "Follow for the money stuff nobody explained in high school."

**On-screen text:** FOLLOW

**Visual:** Creator talking directly to camera, kitchen or dorm background.

---
