# Script 02 — "What a credit score is and the three habits that move it"

Format: vertical 9:16, 45–60 seconds.

## HOOK (0:00–0:02)

- Voiceover: "Your credit score follows you after college. Here's what it actually is."
- On-screen text: WHAT IS A CREDIT SCORE?
- Visual: Talking head, close-up, phone-in-hand energy. Quick cut in.

## BEAT 1

- Voiceover: "A credit score is a number. It tells lenders how risky it looks to lend to you."
- On-screen text: A number lenders look at
- Visual: Phone screen showing a big three-digit number, held to camera.

## BEAT 2

- Voiceover: "Scores usually run from 300 to 850. Higher is generally better."
- On-screen text: 300 → 850
- Visual: Animated bar filling from left to right.

## BEAT 3

- Voiceover: "Habit one: pay every bill on time. Payment history is the single biggest piece of the score."
- On-screen text: 1. Pay on time
- Visual: Calendar screen with recurring payment reminders being checked off.

## BEAT 4

- Voiceover: "Habit two: keep the balance on any credit card low compared to its limit."
- On-screen text: 2. Keep balances low
- Visual: A card balance bar kept short next to a longer limit bar.

## BEAT 5

- Voiceover: "Habit three: let your accounts get older. Length of history counts, so opening and closing cards constantly works against you."
- On-screen text: 3. Let accounts age
- Visual: Timeline graphic stretching from freshman year onward.

## BEAT 6

- Voiceover: "Two more things: checking your own score doesn't hurt it, and student loans count toward it too."
- On-screen text: Checking your own score is fine
- Visual: Quick split screen — a score-check screen, then a loan statement.

## CTA

- Voiceover: "Follow for part three, where roommates and rent get messy."
- On-screen text: Part 3: splitting costs → follow
- Visual: Talking head, pointing up at the follow button.
