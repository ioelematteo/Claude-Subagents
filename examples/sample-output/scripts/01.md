# Script 01 — How to build a budget when your income is irregular

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 45–60s
**Pillar:** Money basics for beginners

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:02)

**Voiceover:** "Your income changes every month. Your budget shouldn't panic every month."

**On-screen text:** BUDGETING ON IRREGULAR INCOME

**Visual:** Hand holds phone showing a banking app balance that changes as the person walks; quick cut to the same hand dropping a notebook on a desk.

---

## BEAT 1

**Voiceover:** "First, find your floor. Look at your last few months. What's the smallest amount you earned in one month?"

**On-screen text:** STEP 1 — FIND YOUR FLOOR

**Visual:** Notes app or notebook with three months of numbers written down, camera tilting down the list.

---

## BEAT 2

**Voiceover:** "Build your budget on that smallest number. That way a slow month doesn't break the plan."

**On-screen text:** BUDGET ON THE SMALLEST MONTH

**Visual:** Hands writing a short list of expenses under the lowest number; split-screen of a small paycheck and a calm, checked-off list.

---

## BEAT 3

**Voiceover:** "Cover the basics first. Rent, food, phone, transport. These get paid before anything else."

**On-screen text:** BASICS FIRST

**Visual:** Four items circled on a piece of paper, one by one, in marker.

---

## BEAT 4

**Voiceover:** "Then decide what to do with extra. Good month? Send some to savings. Tight month? You already planned for it."

**On-screen text:** GOOD MONTH → SAVINGS

**Visual:** Phone transferring a small amount to a savings account; then a person shrugging and closing the laptop.

---

## BEAT 5

**Voiceover:** "You don't need an app. A notes file and five minutes at the end of each month is enough."

**On-screen text:** 5 MINUTES A MONTH

**Visual:** Timer on phone set to five minutes; thumbs typing a quick monthly update in a notes app.

---

## CTA

**Voiceover:** "Write down your lowest month this week. That number is your budget."

**On-screen text:** START WITH YOUR LOWEST MONTH

**Visual:** Notebook page with the lowest number circled; hand sets the pen down.

---

**Estimated runtime:** 52 seconds
