2. ADVICE NEEDING A DISCLAIMER
- "Build your budget on that smallest number... Cover the basics first. Rent, food, phone, transport. These get paid before anything else." — scripts/01.md. Required: not financial/tax advice; if the floor is below basics, the budget will not balance; gig/self-employment income may require setting aside taxes or estimated payments. Failing scenario: a gig worker spends the floor on rent and food, then owes a tax bill. Fix: add a tax set-aside step and a "if the floor does not cover basics, seek more income/assistance" caveat.
- "Good month? Send some to savings." — scripts/01.md. Required: not investment advice; no promised returns; savings vs. investing distinction. Failing scenario: viewer treats a small savings transfer as an investment plan.
- Credit habits: "pay every bill on time," "keep the balance... low," "let your accounts get older." — scripts/02.md. Required: not financial/credit advice; scoring models and lenders vary; paying in full avoids interest; carrying a balance is not required; late payments can be reported; consult a lender or credit counselor.
- "checking your own score doesn't hurt it." — scripts/02.md. Required: soft vs. hard inquiry qualification; some services or actions may differ.
- "student loans count toward it too." — scripts/02.md. Required: loan type/status varies; deferment/forbearance reporting varies; check the actual credit report.
- "Split rent by room size. Split utilities by headcount. Split groceries by receipt" and "Write it down before you sign the lease." — scripts/03.md. Required: not legal advice; the lease and local law control; joint and several liability may let a landlord collect full rent from any one tenant; a side agreement may not bind the landlord; get written landlord approval and consult legal aid. Failing scenario: roommates split rent by room size, but the landlord still collects the full amount from one tenant.
- "Split utilities by headcount." — scripts/03.md. Required: headcount is a proxy, not a measure of use; if one roommate is away or uses more heat/electricity, they should agree on a different formula.
